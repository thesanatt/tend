Made-up Code 1.1. Crime victim compensation

(1) The board pays the reasonable expenses of a victim of a crime committed in this state, as this section provides.
(2) A claim must be filed within three years of the crime. The board may extend the time for good cause.
(3) The maximum award is $25,000 for each claim. An award for counseling is not more than $5,000.
(4) A provider shall not bill the victim for a sexual assault medical forensic examination. The provider sends the bill to the board.
(5) A victim is not required to report the crime to a law enforcement agency to have the examination paid for.
(6) The board decides a claim within 90 days after it has the documents it asked for, and tells the claimant in writing.
(7) A claimant who disagrees with a decision may ask the board to hear the claim again within 30 days of the decision.
