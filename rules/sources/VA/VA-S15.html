Contact Us

In-person assistance is available by appointment only. Please email us at appointment@vvf.virginia.gov with your name, telephone number and what type of assistance is needed. Someone from our office will contact you within two business days.

 

Please send all postal mail to:
Virginia Victims Fund
PO Box 26927
Richmond VA 23261

 

800-552-4007

Customer service is available to answer calls Monday through Friday, except on state and federal holidays. If you call during non-business hours or are prompted to leave a message, please leave a detailed message that includes your name, phone number, and claim number. Calls will be returned as quickly as possible.
