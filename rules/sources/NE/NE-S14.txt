Other Assistance Available for
Crime Victims
Victims of crime may also receive assistance such
as emergency shelter, crisis intervention,
emotional support, and criminal justice
information by contacting a Victim/Witness or
Domestic Violence/Sexual Assault Center in your
area or by contacting the Nebraska Victim
Advocacy Program.
Contact your local law enforcement agency or
county attorney for more information.
CVR Staff can provide a list of statewide contacts.

What is the Crime Victim’s
Reparations (CVR) program?
The CVR program is a
program which became
effective in 1979 in the State
of Nebraska to help victims
of crimes who have been
injured or have suffered a
financial loss because of the
crime.

ncc.nebraska.gov/crime-victim-reparations

CVR is an application and
approval process. Please reach
out to CVR Staff or a victim
advocate if you have questions
about eligibility and
eligible crime types.
CVR program applications take
time, typically claims are fully
processed within 6-9 months.
Every effort is made to determine
eligibility as soon as possible.
