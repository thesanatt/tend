Neb. Rev. Stat. §81-1809 (1) states that, “A hearing officer shall consider and rule upon any application
made under the Nebraska Crime Victim's Reparations Act within one hundred eighty days after receipt
of all required information related to the crime.”
The key elements here are “receipt of all required information”. The clock does not start until all
information is received. It is true that with some cases, it can take 6-9 months or more to receive all of
the documentation required. This is especially true for bills coming from multiple sources (i.e., several
different physicians) or for care provided over a period of time (i.e., physical therapy, counseling). That
said, claimants can be missing more than bills in their applications and have other supporting materials
missing.
Contact CVR at any time during the process for an update on the status of your claim.
Important Note: If you move or get a new phone number, notify CVR immediately.
