Bureau of Victim Compensation

In the aftermath of crime, the Florida Bureau of Victim Compensation works to ease the financial hardships suffered by crime victims. The process is initiated when a claim form and acceptable proof of crime (some exceptions may apply) are received. Forms used to apply for compensation and other publications are available below.
