RCW 7.68.074 Compensation for loss of or damage to clothing or
footwear. Victims otherwise eligible for compensation under this
chapter may also claim compensation for loss of or damage to the
victim's personal clothing or footwear incurred in the course of
emergency medical treatment for injuries. [2011 c 346 s 406.]
Intent—Retroactive application—Effective date—2011 c 346: See
notes following RCW 7.68.020.
