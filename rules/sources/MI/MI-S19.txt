CRIME VICTIM SERVICES COMPENSATION APPLICATION REFERENCE GUIDE
A separate application should be filed for each person who was injured or is seeking benefits.
The Crime Victim Services Commission (CVSC) Compensation Program may help crime victims and
their families with the financial costs of crime. We may cover costs including medical and dental
treatment, non-medical remedial treatment, counseling, funeral expenses, crime scene cleanup, grief counseling, residential security, relocation expenses, transportation, loss of
earnings or support, and other necessary services not paid by other sources. The program
is administered by the Michigan Department of Health and Human Services.
What are the basic eligibility requirements for the Crime Victim Compensation Program?
• An individual must be emotionally, physically, psychologically, or mentally injured as a
direct result of a crime
• If the victim is a minor, deceased, or mentally incapacitated, a claimant can file a claim for
expenses incurred as a result of the victim’s injuries
• The victim must be injured in the State of Michigan, or another state or country that does not
have a crime victim compensation fund
• The crime must be reported to law enforcement
• If the crime was a sexual assault, then a sexual assault forensic exam may be used in lieu of
reporting to police
• The victim or claimant must cooperate with law enforcement officials in the investigation and
prosecution of the case
• The victim or claimant must seek reimbursement from other available sources (health
insurance, Medicaid, Medicare, disability insurance, etc.)
• File a claim with within five years from the date of crime or discovery, waivers may apply
• The victim or claimant must not be involved in any criminal activity or misconduct at the
time of the crime
IMPORTANT INFORMATION
If your address or phone number changes, it is important that you let us know. The toll-free number for
victims is 877-251-7373.
Staff cannot discuss an application with anyone who is not listed as the victim, a claimant, or an
attorney representing the victim, unless written or verbal permission is provided to CVSC by
an authorized individual. This includes phone inquiries or any other method of
communication. A business, agency, medical facility, or organization cannot apply as a claimant.
If you need help completing this application, please feel free to call our office to speak
with a Crime Victim Compensation program Navigator. You may access our website at
www.michigan.gov/crimevictim to find more information about the Crime Victim Compensation
program.

emotional wellbeing as a result of the crime
Replacement services that, because of the victim's injury or claimant' attendance at a victim
services organization to receive services related to the crime, must be performed by another
person

Who is not eligible for Crime Victim Compensation Program benefits?
• The offender, an accomplice of the offender, or any person engaged in illegal activity at the
time of the crime
• A victim or claimant who fails to cooperate with the investigation, prosecution or with
Crime Victim Services
• A victim who is injured while incarcerated in a federal, state, or local correctional facility
• If resources have covered all the eligible out of pocket expenses
What expenses are not covered by the Crime Victim Compensation Program?
• Damage, repair or loss to some personal property or vehicle, such as a purse or cell phone
• Pain, suffering, or emotional distress damages
• Any expense that is not directly related to the crime
Crime Victim Services is considered the payor of last resort.
• All other available third-party resources (for example, Medicare, Medicaid, personal health
insurance, Workers' Compensation, court-ordered and paid restitution, and settlements)
must meet their legal obligations to pay crime-related expenses
• The Crime Victim Services Commission must be notified that a civil lawsuit is filed in relation
to the crime, if restitution is ordered by the criminal court, or if any party receives the proceeds
of a settlement
