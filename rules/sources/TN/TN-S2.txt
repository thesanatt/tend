CRIMINAL INJURIES COMPENSATION FUND
MAXIMUM EXPENSE AMOUNTS
The Criminal Injuries Compensation Fund is a fund of last resort. There is an overall
limit for expenses the program can cover for claims meeting all eligibility requirements.
The maximum amount is based on the date of the crime.
• For crimes occurring on or after July 1, 2025: 		 $34,100
• For crimes occurring between 7/1/24 and 6/30/25: 		 $32,900
• For crimes occurring between 7/1/17 and 6/30/24: 		 $32,000
Certain reimbursement types have sub-limits that apply to the overall maximum:
• Funeral and burial expenses (eligible parties)		$6,000
• Counseling for eligible persons besides the victim		 $3,500
• Pain and suffering (sexually oriented crimes only)		$3,000
• Crime scene cleanup		 $3,000
• Travel to & from trial (under certain conditions) 		$1,250
(Cumulative & can include up to 4 people)
*Payments to claimants may be subject to DHS liens for child support.
Example:
If a claimant requests reimbursement for funeral/burial plus loss of support, the 		
claimant may receive up to $6,000 for eligible funeral expenses. This reimbursement
leaves up to $28,100 for other non-funeral expenses, such as loss of support.
			

$34,100 - $6,000 = $28,100

If expenses exceed the maximum allowed, the program will prioritize the claimant’s eligible
out of pocket expenses over those due to another party. Lost wages and loss of support will
be calculated and prioritized. Reimbursements to vendors are paid after lost wages. Direct
payments to medical providers are pro-rated at 75% of the balance owed.
The overall maximum is calculated annually and based on a national average of all other
state program limits, then increased by 5%. The sub-limits are set in state law in
T.C.A. § 29-13-107 and can only be updated through legislation.
