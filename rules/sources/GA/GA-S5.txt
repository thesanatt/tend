Our Programs Intro Over time, our programs have grown to meet the needs of victims, such as accessing unclaimed court ordered restitution from an offender, supporting the placement of a memorial for a loved one killed by an impaired driver and more. Crime victims can apply directly for Victims Comp, Officers Initiative, DUI Memorial Sign and Unclaimed Restitution Programs. Providers apply on behalf of victims to the Forensic Interview Program and the Forensic Medical Examination Program. Our Programs Victims Compensation DUI Memorial Sign Forensic Interviews Forensic Medical Examinations Victims Unclaimed Restitution Were you or your business the victim of a crime in Georgia? Did the court order the offender to pay money back to you? Is there a possibility you didn’t collect part or all of those funds? When an offender is sentenced in court, part of their sentence may include paying restitution (money) back to the victim of the crime. When money is unclaimed by a victim or business for more than two years, it is transferred to the Crime Victims Emergency Fund. At that time, the Crime Victims Compensation’s Unclaimed Restitution Program makes attempts to locate and contact the victim or business.
