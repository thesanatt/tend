This enclosure outlines the eligibility criteria for Economic Support benefits.
Lost Wages (victim): This benefit applies to eligible victims who were gainfully
employed at the time of the crime and suffered a loss of income due to the
victimization. To apply for Lost Wages benefits, please provide the following
documentation:
Required Documents

□

Proof of at least 60 days of income prior to the victimization. If you were employed
less than 60 days prior to the victimization, please send proof of income since your
hire date (For example, Pay Stubs, Payroll History, W-2 Form, Federal Tax Return
Transcript, etc.)

Valid Business License/Permit/Occupational License, if applicable (submit if

4. Work dates missed due to the victimization
5. Wages lost

Proof of return to work. If you were out of work more than one (1) week, the doctor
may submit the enclosed Work Release Form OR you need to submit a doctor’s
release stating the length of time you were out of work and the date you were
released to return back to work.
