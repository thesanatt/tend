Arizona provides financial assistance to victims of
crime through the Victim Compensation Program,
administered through your County Attorney’s office.
If you have been victimized by crime, you may apply
for financial assistance for out-of-pocket expenses
incurred as a direct result of the crime.

Victim Compensation is a program
administered by the Arizona
Criminal Justice Commission
(ACJC), in cooperation with your
local County Attorney’s Office. If
you have questions about the
Victim Compensation Program,
please feel free to contact ACJC at:
1-877-668-2252 (Toll-free) or visit
our website at: www.azcjc.gov.
