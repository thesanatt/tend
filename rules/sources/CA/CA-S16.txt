BENEFIT REFERENCE GUIDE

Victims of violent crime may be able to receive up to $70,000 in total compensation to help them recover.
This guide tells you:
• Who can receive benefits • How much they can receive • What information CalVCB needs •

WHO CAN RECEIVE BENEFITS?
A DIRECT VICTIM is physically hurt, threatened with injury, or dies because of a crime.
A DERIVATIVE VICTIM has expenses because of the direct victim’s injury or death. This can be a family member, roommate
or caretaker.
A PAYER is anyone who pays or assumes liability for crime-related expenses.
REIMBURSEMENT TYPE

WHO QUALIFIES?

FUNERAL AND/OR BURIAL

MAXIMUM BENEFIT
Up to $12,818

Examples of What We Need:
• Proof that the victim’s death was the result of a qualifying crime.
• Receipts from funeral or burial expenses. Providers may also bill CalVCB directly.
MEDICAL AND DENTAL EXPENSES

Up to $70,000

Examples of What We Need:
• Proof from the crime report or medical/dental records that medical/dental services are required.
• Proof that medical devices were taken, lost, damaged, destroyed or are now needed because of the crime.
• Note from a physician if in-home services are required that states the length of time care is needed, and that care from a family
member or another person is medically necessary.
• Dental bills must be submitted on an American Dental Association Claim form.
MENTAL HEALTH TREATMENT

Up to 60 sessions and $10,000 as
detailed below

Session Limits:
• 60 sessions
Session Limits:
• 60 sessions if the direct victim died because of the crime.
• 50 sessions if the derivative victim was a minor at the time of the crime.
• 50 sessions if the derivative victim was the primary caretaker of the direct victim at the time of the crime. This is limited to $10,000
total for two caretakers.
• 30 sessions if the derivative victim does not meet the above criteria. This is limited to $5,000.
• 30 sessions if the derivative victim became the caretaker of a minor victim after the crime. This is limited to $5,000 and must be for
the benefit of the direct victim.
Examples of What We Need:
• Itemized receipts for mental health treatment received. Providers may also bill CalVCB directly.
• A completed Mental Health Billing Intake form from the provider with their first bill.
• Proof that insurance has been billed, if applicable.
GUIDE, SIGNAL, OR SERVICE DOG

Up to $10,000

Examples of What We Need:
• Receipts for costs to buy or treat a guide, signal, or service dog that was injured or died because of the crime.

Up to $70,000

INCOME LOSS
Examples of What We Need:
• A disability statement from a medical or mental health provider.
• Proof from a medical provider that a parent or guardian needed to be with a minor in the hospital.
• Proof of income (tax return, pay stub, or statement from an employer).

SUPPORT LOSS

Examples of What We Need:
• Proof that the person was legally dependent on the victim at the time of the crime (birth or marriage certificate, adoption records,
child support records, or Social Security documents).
• Proof of income (tax return, pay stub, or statement from an employer).
HUMAN TRAFFICKING COMPENSATION

Up to $10,000 per year for a maximum
of two years

Examples of What We Need:
• Proof of income loss as a direct result of human trafficking through either:
◊ Law enforcement report, or
◊ Sworn statement under penalty of perjury from the applicant, a human trafficking case worker, a licensed attorney, or a witness.
• A completed Human Trafficking Compensation form.
JOB RETRAINING OR SIMILAR EMPLOYMENT SERVICES

Up to $70,000

Examples of What We Need:
• An evaluation from the California Department of Rehabilitation.
Up to $3,418

RELOCATION
Examples of What We Need:
• Proof from law enforcement that relocation is needed for a victim’s safety.
• Proof from a mental health provider that moving is needed for a victim’s emotional well-being.
• Receipts for relocation expenses such as rent, utility deposits, or moving expenses.
• A completed rental verification form and a copy of the lease.

Up to $1,000

HOME SECURITY
Examples of What We Need:
• Proof of expenses needed as a direct result of the crime.
• Receipts for home security expenses.
HOME MODIFICATION FOR PARTIALLY OR
TOTALLY DISABLED VICTIMS

Up to $70,000

Examples of What We Need:
• Proof from a doctor that modifications are medically needed because of the crime.
• A written report from a home modification specialist.
• The contractor’s detailed description of the work to be done, payment plan, and a copy of their license.
• Receipts for home modification expenses.
VEHICLE MODIFICATION OR PURCHASE

Up to $30,000

Examples of What We Need:
• Proof from a doctor that modifying or buying a modified vehicle is medically needed because of the crime.
• A statement from the victim explaining if public transportation is available.
• A bill or estimate showing the cost for modifying or buying the vehicle.
• A report from a mobility specialist.
• A copy of the driver’s license for the person who will operate the vehicle.
Up to $1,709

CRIME SCENE CLEANUP
Examples of What We Need:
• Proof that the crime occurred in a residence or vehicle.
• Receipts for cleanup of a home or vehicle by an approved waste cleanup company.
