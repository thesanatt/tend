A claim will be opened and assigned a case number. The Commission will request a police report from the reporting law enforcement agency. An Investigator will be assigned to review your application. You will receive a written administrative decision and order either awarding or denying compensation. Questions? Contact the Crime Victim Compensation Commission office at (808) 587-1143 or cvcc@hawaii.rr.com .
