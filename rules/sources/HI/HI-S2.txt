Crime-Related Expenses – Crime Victim Compensation Commission Skip to Main Content Home / Crime-Related Expenses The Crime Victim Compensation Commission (CVCC) is a “payor of last resort” and may pay compensation after all other sources have been exhausted. An award may be reduced by amounts received from medical insurance, Workers’ Compensation, Motor Vehicle Insurance, Civil Suits, Temporary Disability Insurance or Restitution from the offender.
