Crime Victim Compensation Program Application Instructions
Before completing the application for compensation, please read the instructions and the definitions of the types of
losses available for compensation. If you need assistance, please contact the Division of Victim Services.

General Information/Instructions:

Please type or print clearly in blue or black ink.
Attach all bills, receipts, estimates, or provide contact information for verification of the expense amounts.
If you have not received a letter or phone call from our office within 30 days after mailing in your application,
please call 307-777-7200 or send an e-mail to inquire about the status of your claim. If you change your address,
you MUST notify the division.
You do not need to wait for your case to go to trial or for the defendant to be apprehended before applying for
compensation. If you do not know the status of your case, you have the right to contact law enforcement or the
prosecutor’s office.
If you have not been informed of your rights as a victim of crime, please contact our agency.
A federal crime is an incident occurring within a national park, military base, an Indian reservation, etc. or is
investigated by federal law enforcement agencies, such as the F.B.I.
The maximum benefits for a claim may not exceed $15,000 and can be paid for 2 years from the date of the
crime. Mental health expenses can be paid for 3 years from the date of the crime.
The law does not allow compensation for property loss or pain and suffering.

A person is eligible for compensation if:





The crime occurred in Wyoming.
The injury or death occurred as a result of a crime.
The person was attempting to prevent the commission of a criminal act or apprehend a person committing a
crime.
The person was assisting law enforcement, or a victim of a crime.

Types of Compensation Available:














MEDICAL/MENTAL HEALTH- The cost of medical and dental services, mental health counseling, prescriptions,
prosthetic devices, eyeglass, dentures, physical therapy and services rendered in accordance with any method of
healing recognized by the law of this state. Elderly victims of fraud are eligible for mental health counseling only.
LOSS OF EARNINGS- The victim must be employed on the date of the crime. Victims who are self-employed may
be required to submit additional information for verifying lose wages. Loss of earnings is compensated at the
federal minimum wage or actual loss, whichever is less at the time of the crime. Collateral sources such as
vacation or sick pay must be used before lost wages will be compensated.
LOSS OF SUPPORT- Victim must be employed on the date of the crime. Loss of support may be awarded to
dependents of a deceased victim or a victim who is incapacitated and was employed at the time of the crime
and is no longer contributing to the support of the family. Loss of support is compensated at the federal
minimum wage or actual loss, whichever is less at the time of the crime.
FUNERAL/BURIAL- Funeral related expenses such as burial or cremation costs, headstone, flowers, clergy costs,
musical/soloist fees, cultural exchanges etc. Maximum benefit $7,000.
OTHER EXPENSES- Travel costs, replacement of personal items taken as evidence, and crime scene clean-up
(must be filed with homeowners/renters insurance first). Attach itemized lists/bills/receipts.
RELOCATION EXPENSES- Expenses to relocate a victim to another community or state due to a threat of
personal injury. Expenses for relocation apply only if the crime scene was the home of the victim (domestic
violence, homicide, and sexual assaults only). Examples: U-Haul, gas, mileage, per diem. Threat must be verified
in writing by law enforcement, prosecuting attorney, or any agency with statutory authority.
CATASTROPHIC INJURY- May be awarded to a victim if they have sustained partial or permanent disability (loss
of eyesight, hearing, or limbs) as a result of the crime. A physician must verify this loss. In addition, a
catastrophic award can apply to the cost of an air ambulance. The maximum catastrophic award is $10,000 in
addition to the overall award of $15,000.
ASSOCIATED VICTIMS- If immediate family or household members, other than the victim, are seeking
compensation for counseling or other economic loss, please request an associated victim’s application from your
victim advocate.

IMPORTANT NOTE: Crime Victims Compensation is a payer of last resort. Before compensation can be
considered for award, all collateral sources must be exhausted. Examples of collateral sources are
Social Security, health insurance, life insurance, Medicaid, Medicare, vacation, or sick pay.
