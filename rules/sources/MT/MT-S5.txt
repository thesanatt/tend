Request a Claim Form – Montana Department of Justice Skip to content Office of Victim Services Request a Claim Form DOJ Webmaster 2026-10-02T11:19:16-06:00 Request a Claim Form The Montana Crime Victim Compensation Program assists victims of violent crimes who are injured or killed as a direct result of a crime. A claim can only be filed by: The victim – a person who has suffered direct physical or emotional harm or death as a result of a crime is referred to as the primary victim. A parent or legal guardian of a victim who is a minor. The spouse or next of kin of a deceased victim. Exceptions may apply. Benefits may be awarded to: Non-residents injured in Montana. Montana residents injured in a state or country in which the victim compensation program does not compensate non-residents. How to Request a Claim Form There are several ways to obtain a Crime Victim Compensation claim form. You may request a claim form from any law enforcement agency, hospital, victim assistance program, or by contacting the Office of Victim Services. The Office of Victim Services can be reached by phone at (406) 444-3653 or 1-800-498-6455. You may also submit the online request below to receive a claim form by mail or download and print the Victim Compensation Claim Form. Crime Victim Compensation Program P.O. Box 201410 Helena, MT 59620-1410 Please note: Be sure your claim form is signed and dated before you mail to the CVC program.
