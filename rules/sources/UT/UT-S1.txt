Victim Compensation | Utah Office for Victims of Crime Skip to content Safety Exit Victim Compensation Help for Victims The Utah Office for Victims of Crime (UOVC) provides financial compensation for crime related expenses to those who have suffered physical or psychological injury or death as a direct result of a crime. This includes family members and dependents of deceased victims or representatives acting on behalf of victims.
