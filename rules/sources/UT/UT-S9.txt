Contact Us | Utah Office for Victims of Crime Skip to content Safety Exit Contact Us About UOVC The Utah Office for Victims of Crime is committed to providing clear information and responsive assistance. Whether you are a victim seeking help, a provider with program questions, or a member of the public looking for information, we welcome your inquiry and will work to connect you with the appropriate staff or resources. Accessibility & Language Assistance UOVC is committed to ensuring our services and information are accessible to everyone. If you need materials in an alternative format, language interpretation, translation assistance, or accommodations to participate in a meeting or training, please contact us or indicate your needs in the form above. We will work with you to provide reasonable accommodations. Important: Immediate Help If you are in immediate danger, please call 911.
