Sexual Assault Services Providers | Utah Office for Victims of Crime Skip to content Safety Exit Sexual Assault Services Providers Billing This page explains how sexual assualt services providers can submit billing to the Utah Office for Victims of Crime (UOVC) for eligible sexual assault related services. Reminder : Providers must bill all available insurance or other payment sources before submitting claims to UOVC. Payment Information Payment amounts for sexual assault forensic exams and related services are based on the UOVC payment tables. View the payment tables for eligible services . Billing for sexual assault forensic exams or related services must be submitted within one year from the date of the service. This includes submissions using the Fast Track Reimbursement Form or provider billing. Payment in Full Requirement A sexual assault exam service provider who accepts payment from UOVC agrees to accept that payment as payment in full on behalf of the patient and may not attempt to collect additional payment from the patient.
