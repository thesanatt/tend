Crime Related Counseling Expenses
DCVC provides reimbursement for trauma (generally
considered as a medical expense) only when such service
is rendered by a professional who is licensed in a specialty
which includes mental health counseling; this includes
LMSW (when not practicing independently) LPC, LMFT,
LCSW, LISW, Psychiatrist, Psychologist, and MD.
NOTE: DCVC’s mental health policy provides an
incremental approach to outpatient mental health sessions.
The provider must have received specific training in
evidence-based treatment that has been shown to be
effective in meeting the needs of crime victims.
NOTE: DCVC pays after health insurance.

Crime Related Lost Wages
You must meet the four (4) criteria:
1.

Employment: The victim must have been employed at the
time of the crime,

2.

Missed time from work: The victim must have
missed two (2) consecutive weeks (14 days) from work as
a direct result of the crime,

3.

Reportable income: Reimbursement is based on
reportable income, and

4.

Disabled: The victim must be under the care of a treating
physician.

Crime Related Lost Wages
(You were self employed at the time of the crime)
1.

Employment: The victim must have been employed at the
time of the crime,

2.

Missed time from work: The victim must have missed two
(2) consecutive weeks (14 days) from work as a direct result
of the crime,

3.

Reportable income: Reimbursement is based on reportable
income, and

4.

Disabled: The victim must be under the care of a treating
Physician.

THEN: You will need to provide
The following documents must be submitted:



DCVC Employer’s Report
DCVC Physician’s Disability Report
Copy of your last two pay stubs (prior to the crime date).

NOTE: Because DCVC is a payer of last resort, all sources
such as annual or sick leave, long/short term disability,
SSA/SSI must be exhausted before DCVC will consider lost
wage benefits.

1.

Disability:
DCVC Physician’s Disability Report (will be required to

2.

Employment:
 DCVC Self-Employment Verification of Lost Wages
form.
 A copy of your Business License (if applicable), or
 Documentation showing you were receiving income,
from the business, at the time of the crime.

3.

Reportable Income: (lost wages are calculated using
information for the year of the crime)
 Tax Return Transcript from the IRS

NOTE: Because DCVC is a payer of last resort, all sources such as
annual or sick leave, long/short term disability, SSA/SSI must be
exhausted before DCVC will consider lost wage benefits.
NOTE: Payment for lost wage benefits are limited to one half of the
overall compensation award amount and a 12 month disability
period.
