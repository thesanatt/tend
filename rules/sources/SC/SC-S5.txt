The eligibility requirements to receive services from DCVC are: DCVC Eligibility Criteria The crime must occur in South Carolina The victim must sustain a direct injury - (Physical or Psychological) South Carolina law requires DCVC to consider contributory or illegal behavior when making eligibility determinations The crime must be reported to law enforcement within 48 hours (May be waived for good cause) The victim/claimant must cooperate with DCVC and Law Enforcement The claim must be filed within 180 days (May be waived for good cause) The claim must be filed within 4 years of the incident. In addition, the time for filing may be extended four years after a diagnosis of injury Crime Victim Services FAQs The Office of the Attorney General provides programs to help support crime victims.
