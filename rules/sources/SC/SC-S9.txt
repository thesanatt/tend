Legislation

Pursuant to Section 16-3-1350

South Carolina law which follows the guidance
of the Federal Violence Against Women Act
Statute, provides that victims of assault in the
State of South Carolina may request, at no cost
to them, a forensic examination for sexual
assault, regardless of their involvement with
law enforcement.

DCVC
Office of the Attorney General

Compensation
Benefits for
Victims of
Sexual Assault

Sexual Assault Program: DCVC is the Sole
Reimbursement Provider for Forensic Medical
Examinations in S.C. Health Care Providers will
bill DCVC directly; all reimbursements are from a
fee schedule and are considered payment in full.
Any injuries sustained beyond the sexual assault
such as additional physical or psychological
injuries will be the victim’s responsibility.
However, the victim may complete a Crime
Victims’ Compensation Application to assist with
the additional needs.
Please Note: Law
enforcement will be involved as the crime victim
compensation application is processed, unless the
victim files anonymously.
