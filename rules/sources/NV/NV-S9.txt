Application for Victim of Crime Compensation Instructions
Being impacted by a crime can be overwhelming. Please take your time completing this claim form.
Help is available if you need it.
You May Be Eligible for Help If:
•

The crime occurred in Nevada, and

•

The crime was reported to law enforcement or another appropriate authority, and

•

You are considered a “victim” under Nevada law—meaning you were harmed physically,
emotionally, or ﬁnancially because of a violent crime, and

•

Your application was submitted within the time allowed by law.

Expenses That May Be Covered
The Program may help with certain crime-related expenses, including:
•

Medical, hospital, and ambulance bills

•

Counseling or mental health services

•

Lost wages or survivor beneﬁts (A social security number must be included in the application)

•

Funeral or burial expenses

•

Crime-scene cleanup

•

Child care, relocation, or home health care

•

Vision care, physical therapy, or chiropractic services

•

Property damage related to qualifying hate crimes (limited)

Most beneﬁts have limits. In general, the total award cannot exceed $35,000, although some services—
such as counseling and crime-scene cleanup—may be covered beyond that amount.
Important to Know
The Victims Compensation Program is the payer of last resort. This means we must consider payments
from other sources, such as insurance or restitution, before determining your award.

Filling Your Claim
•

Please print clearly and complete only the sections that apply to you.

•

Provide a safe phone number, email address, or mailing address where we can reach you.

•

Submit any documents you have now—you may send additional information later.

•

Be sure to sign the required authorization and release forms.

Need Help?
If you would like assistance with your claim, you may contact the VOCP oﬃce or a local victim service
program listed on our website at Victims of Crime (https://www.voc.nv.gov/ ).
We are committed to reviewing your claim as carefully and quickly as possible. You are not alone, and
support is available.
