Read Before Downloading VOCP Application A fully completed, signed application is required before VOCP assistance can be reviewed. The applicant must complete each section of the application to the best of  their ability. Incomplete or unreadable applications will not be considered. The application must be completed in black or blue ink. Do Not Use Pencil. NOTE: An application cannot be approved unless the crime has been reported to the police or other proper authority. A Police Report should be provided with the application whenever possible. If an applicant is unable to obtain an official police report, they may submit the application without a police report, and the VOCP will attempt to obtain a report for the applicant. If the VOCP is unable, after written request, to obtain a police report based on the information provided in the application, the application will be denied. Assistance with Application If you need assistance with completing an application, you can request help at the Victims of Crime Program office. The VOCP provides resources and services, in collaboration with the Vegas Strong Resiliency and Justice Center, that are available to help victims, their families and others directly. Applications are available in English or Spanish. Completed applications can be emailed to vocp-applications@dcfs.nv.gov The Victims of Crime Program office is located at 500 E. Warm Springs Road, Suite 100, Las Vegas, NV 89119, and open Monday – Friday 8:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m., Phone (702) 486-2740.
