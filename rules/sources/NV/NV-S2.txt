VOCP Benefits and Covered Expenses Skip Nav VOC Benefits & Expenses The Victims of Crime Program goal is to assist eligible victims and their families cope and recover from the physical, emotional, and financial impact of violent crime. The VOCP assists victims with a variety of expenses that occur because of the crime. VOCP may assist with crime-related expenses such as: Childcare costs incurred because of the crime Crime scene cleanup Emergency Shelter and Relocation costs Funeral and burial expenses Home Health Care Home Security Repair Hospital and Ambulance bills Insurance co-payments Loss of support, for dependents of a deceased victim Medical and Dental treatment Medically necessary equipment (e.g., wheelchair) Mental Health Counseling Prescription Medication Vision prosthetics and eyeglass replacement Wage or income loss VOCP cannot pay the following crime- related expenses: Damages for pain and suffering Lost or stolen property or cash Property damage Any expense not directly related to the crime Any expense payable by insurance or any other source Claims are limited to $35,000 The VOCP can only reimburse crime-related expenses not covered by other sources. There are limits on individual benefits and no claim may exceed $35,000. Scroll to Top
