Address Confidentiality Program | Department of State Skip to main content Address Confidentiality Program Address Confidentiality Program Our Mission As part of an overall safety plan for victims of domestic violence, the Address Confidentiality Program helps victims shield their addresses from their abusers. Core Services We provide a substitute mailing address that victims may use in lieu of a home, school and/or work address.  The Address Confidentiality Program provides a free mail forwarding service and accepts legal notices on behalf of participants. Who We Serve Victims of domestic violence and their household members who have moved to a location unknown to their abusers.
