Forensic Rape Exam Direct Reimbursement Program

OVS can directly reimburse medical providers for forensic rape exams when sexual assault survivors do not have private health insurance or choose not to use their insurance. 

Billing Guidance

OVS laws governing reimbursement for FRE services have recently changed. FRE claims received by the agency on or after February 3, 2026 will be paid according to the OVS fee schedule for services rendered and the NYS Department of Health Formulary File for pharmaceuticals. Providers should review the Provider Billing Manual for more information about billing and reimbursement. 

 

What is a forensic rape exam? 

The Office of Victim Services directly reimburses medical providers for forensic rape examinations (FREs) if victims of sexual assault do not have access to private health insurance or chose not to use their private health care insurance for the examination. This measure is an exception to the agency’s payer of last resort rule and provides for the personal privacy of victims. 

An exam may also include collection and preservation of evidence in a Sexual Offense Evidence Collection Kit (SOECK), which can assist law enforcement if an individual decides to report the crime. Reporting to police is not mandatory, however, and should never prevent someone from seeking medical care.
