Safety Planning

Safe at Home is only one part of your personal safety plan. Some additional things you should consider are school attendance, your place of employment, social media, and the use of technology. Your safety plan should be reviewed periodically to be sure it still meets your needs. For assistance with safety planning, contact a local domestic violence advocate. Online resources regarding safety planning can be found here https://www.thehotline.org/help/path-to-safety/.
