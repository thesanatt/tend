Skip to main content Iowa Safe At Home Safe at Home is an address confidentiality program that provides participants with a substitute address. Who Survivors of domestic violence, sexual assault, trafficking, stalking, and assault. What This program helps survivors improve their lives with the following services: substitute address. mail forwarding service. confidential voter registration and absentee voting. The participants' first-class, legal, certified mail, prescriptions and government packages arrive at a substitute address and are then forwarded to the participants' confidential addresses. Why Prevention of participants' addresses from appearing on public records. How Download and complete the application , then mail it to Safe At Home P.O. Box 959 Des Moines, IA 50304 If you have further questions about the program, please refer to our brochure , FAQ , or contact us at safeathome@iowa.gov or 515-725-7233.
