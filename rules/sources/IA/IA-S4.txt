Crime Victim Compensation Program Quick Reference Guide for Primary Victims

Victim of a violent offense committed in Iowa.
Including, but not limited to:

Who

• Homicide
• Assault
• Domestic Abuse
• Sexual Assault
• OWI, Hit and Run, Reckless Driving or Boating
• Arson or Burglary of Victim's residence

Crime-related Benefits we can consider:

What

What
Not

•Medical $25,000 maximum
•Funeral and burial $7,500 maximum
•Counseling $5,000 maximum
•Lost wages due to injuries (physical or emotional) up to $6,000
•Lost wages to attend criminal court proceedings up to $1,000
•Crime Scene Clean-up of victim's residence up to $1,000
•Travel/lodging up to $1,000
•Emergency Relocation $1,000 per lifetime
•Residential Security up to $500
•Clothing held as evidence $200

Non-eligible crimes and expenses include:
• Theft, forgery, and other financial crimes, and dog bites
• Auto damages, towing expenses, and attorney fees

Now

When

How

• Victims can apply immediately after the crime and upto 2 years after the
crime date. If beyond 2 years from the crime, please contact the office.
• Criminal charges do NOT have to be filed for eligibility.

To request an application or to complete one, call us at 800-373-5044 or
fill one out online at https://www.iowaattorneygeneral.gov/for-crimevictims/crime-victim-compensation-program
