ABOUT US
The Crime Victims Compensation
Program provides financial
assistance and reimbursement to
victims of crime and their families
with crime related expenses.
A maximum of $25,000 per claim
may be paid for necessary crime
related services. Certain expenses
within the claim may be subject to
individual sub-limits.

WHO CAN APPLY?
To qualify for compensation, you must:
• Have suffered a personal injury as a
result of a crime that occurred in
D.C., or a family member of a victim
of crime
• Report the crime within 7 days
(some exceptions apply)
• Apply for assistance within 1 year of
the date the crime occurred

COVERED EXPENSES
The Program can reimburse for
expenses such as:
• Medical/Dental
• Counseling
• Funeral bills
• Lost wages
• Loss of support
• Transportation
• Emergency temporary housing
• Moving and relocation
• Crime scene clean-up
*This is not an exhaustive list*

DOCUMENTS NEEDED

• Other documents may be
