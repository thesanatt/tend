§ 4–561.03. Payment for PERKs. | D.C. Law Library Toggle mobile menu Code of the District of Columbia § 4–561.03. Payment for PERKs. The hospital shall not bill a sexual assault victim for costs associated with a PERK, including the costs of the PERK, its administration, and the processing of the PERK or any biological specimens.
