Crime Victims Compensation Program | District of Columbia Courts Crime Victims Compensation Program Last Updated
            : 02/13/2026 Overview The Crime Victims Compensation Program (CVCP) provides financial assistance and/or reimbursement for crime related expenses. The Program is funded by fines and fees paid to the DC Courts as well as by a Victims of Crime Act (VOCA) grant. You may be able to receive assistance with things like: Funeral and burial costs Medical and mental health bills Lost wages and loss of support Cleaning up the crime scene Emergency shelter or moving to a safer place View the video below to learn more.
