MA SEXUAL ASSAULT EVIDENCE
COLLECTION KIT (MSAECK)
PAYMENT OF RELATED EXPENSES
In Massachusetts, survivors of sexual
violence who receive a forensic
exam should not receive a bill
(including co-pay) for the exam
and/or hospital visit related to the
assault.
If you have insurance and no privacy
concerns, the hospital may bill the
insurance for the emergency room
visit and other related costs. If you
do not have insurance or wish to not
have your insurance billed, all
qualified forensic medical exam
costs should be submitted to the
Victim Compensation Fund. If you
have concerns about billing, contact
the hospital’s billing department.
If you are charged for any exam
costs or costs that don’t qualify as a
directly associated cost, you may
submit a claim through the MA
Victim Compensation Fund for
reimbursement. Please contact the
Victim Compensation Fund at
617-727-2200 for any related
questions.

About the MSAECK

Toxicology Testing

What is the MSAECK?

A toxicology screen will indicate
whether or not a substance is found
in urine or blood at the time of kit
collection. Because substances can
leave the body quickly, a negative
result does not mean that you were
definitely not drugged; it simply
shows that a substance was not
present when the kit was collected.
Please be aware that prescriptions,
over-the-counter medications, and
recreational drugs will be detected
by the screen.

The MSAECK is a standardized tool
used for collecting evidence in cases
of sexual assault.
What is done with the MSAECK?
If you report this assault to law
enforcement, the MSAECK will be
processed at a state or Boston police
crime lab as soon as it is transferred
there. If you decide to not report, the
MSAECK will be stored by the police
department in the area the incident
occurred; it will be transported to a
crime lab if the assault is reported at
any point.
What will the MSAECK tell me?
Testing of the MSAECK will
document whether any physical
evidence, including DNA, is present
on your body or clothing.
Will I get my clothes back from
the MSAECK?
Any clothing or other personal items
collected as part of the MSAECK will
not be returned to you.
Both the MSAECK and toxicology kit
will become property of the
jurisdiction where the assault
occurred. If the assault occurred out
of state, the kit(s) will be transported
to the police department/crime lab,
depending on the other state’s
protocol.

IF THE ASSAULT WAS REPORTED TO
THE POLICE:
If toxicology testing was done (to
detect drugs you may have been
given without your knowledge), you
can contact the police department of
the town where the assault occurred
or the District Attorney’s Office for
the test results.
IF THE ASSAULT WAS NOT
REPORTED TO THE POLICE:
If toxicology testing was done (to
detect drugs you may have been
given without your knowledge), you
can call 866-463-3799 with your
MSAECK number as soon as you feel
comfortable, and an advocate can
help you track the kit as well as find
out the results approximately 12
weeks after testing.
