The Address Confidentiality Program (ACP) protects survivors of domestic violence, sexual assault, rape, and stalking as well as some legally protected healthcare providers. Survivors often move to get away from perpetrators of violence. The Address Confidentiality Program makes their new address harder to find. The ACP is a mail forwarding service that gives you a protected address to use with state and municipal agencies. This keeps your real address out of public records.
