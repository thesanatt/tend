Which crimes are covered by the Rape Victim Assistance Program? What if I receive a bill for my forensic exam? If you receive a bill for the cost of a forensic medical exam, please contact our office. By law, medical providers cannot bill you or your insurance for the forensic exam. Does the Rape Victim Assistance Program cover all hospital and physician expenses? No. The program is only authorized to cover the expenses related to the forensic exam. Is financial help available for medical expenses beyond the cost of the forensic exam?? The state Victim Compensation Program helps eligible survivors of violent crime and families of homicide victims pay for expenses including medical care, counseling, lost wages and funeral services. To learn more about the program and start an application, visit ncdps.gov/4victims. Which crimes are covered by the Rape Victim Assistance Program?
