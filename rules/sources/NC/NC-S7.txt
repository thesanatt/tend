Office of Victim Compensation Services | NC DPS Office of Victim Compensation Services Resources Are Available to Help Victims of Violent Crime Office of Victim Compensation Services provides financial assistance that helps survivors of violent crime and the family members of homicide victims. No amount of money can erase the trauma and grief that follows a violent crime, but this aid can support recovery. Our staff is here to help. All services are free and confidential. Contact Us: 1-800-826-6200 SVG Victim Compensation Program Survivors of violent crime can apply for financial assistance for eligible expenses such as medical care and lost wages. Families of homicide victims may submit funeral costs. SVG Rape Victim Assistance Program State law instructs medical providers who perform the forensic medical examination to refrain from billing the victim. They must instead bill our program. SVG Crime Victim Compensation Commission The members of the commission meet quarterly to review applications and determine eligibility for compensation. SVG Provider Resources Access on-demand training, billing FAQs and other tools for direct service providers and victim advocates. Request brochures and training. Our impact, by the numbers: More than 2,400 calls for support answered each month More than 300 new applications reviewed each month 2,449 applications approved from July 2023 to June 2024 More than $9.8M paid from July 2023 to June 2024
