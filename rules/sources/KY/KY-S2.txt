49.290 Nonresident victims of criminal acts occurring in Kentucky -- Limits on

"Victim" shall also include nonresidents of this state who suffer losses as a direct
result of criminal acts occurring within this state.
This section shall be operative only during those time periods during which the
Crime Victims Compensation Board determines that federal funds are available to
the state for the compensation of victims of crime.
Effective: June 29, 2021
