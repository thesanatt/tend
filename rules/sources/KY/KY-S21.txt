EMERGENCY AWARD REQUEST POLICY
Pursuant to KRS 49.360, a claimant may file for an emergency award and receive up to
$1,000.00 upon a showing that: (1) the claim is one that will likely be awarded; and, (2) the
Claimant will endure an undue hardship if an immediate payment is denied.
Claimants may submit one (1) emergency award request. If it is denied, the order denying
the emergency award cannot be appealed and the claim shall proceed normally until it is fully
adjudicated. Multiple award requests shall only be considered by the Board in the most
extraordinary circumstances, subject to the Board’s discretion.
Effective Date: January 14, 2025
