REQUIREMENTS

The Office of Claims and Appeals –
Crime Victims Compensation Board
(CVCB) administers the Crime Victims
Compensation Fund and considers
payment or reimbursement for expenses
related to crimes occurring in Kentucky
when no other resources are available.
A claimant must:
Be an innocent victim of a crime or
some conduct that could be charged
as a crime and have no other means
of payment.
Or be a surviving family member or
personal representative of the victim;
dependent on principal support from
the victim; be legally responsible for
the medical or funeral expenses of the
victim; or any person acting as the
primary caregiver for the victim.
Cooperate with requests made by the
Board for evidence to support the
claim. Conviction is not required.
REDUCTION OF BENEFITS
The CVCB is the payer of last resort.
Awards must be reduced by amounts
already received or to be received from
other sources like insurance or workers’
comp claims, legal settlements, and/or
restitution.

CLAIM PROCESS
Claim forms are available at kycc.ky.gov.
Forms are reviewed to ensure all
necessary information is included.
The Claimant is notified if additional
information or documentation is required.
Staff review, gather pertinent information
and verify submitted claim.
The claim is assigned to a board member
who makes a recommendation for either
approval or denial of an award to the full
board.
Staff notifies claimant of
recommendation. Claimant has 15 days to
register disagreement.
Board renders a final decision
Claimant receives a copy of the final order.
APPEAL OF A DENIED CLAIM
An appeal may be filed in Franklin Circuit Court
or the circuit court in the county where the
claim occurred.

The CVCB pays providers directly on behalf of the
claimant for crime-related expenses, and can
consider reimbursement to the claimant for
crime-related out-of-pocket expenses.
The maximum award per victim cannot exceed
$50,000; the maximum award for funeral/burial
expenses cannot exceed $10,000, which is
included in the $50,000 cap.

PAYMENT
PROCESS
The time required for processing claims depends
on numerous factors, including the
cooperativeness of the claimant, timely receipt of
requested documentation, the complexity of
issues related to the crime, as well as any issues
resulting from the criminal trial.
The CVCB can consider additional bills after an
initial claim has been filed, as well as emergency
payments up to $1,000. When applicable,
emergency awards are subtracted from the
$50,000 cap on all awards.
Collection agencies are prohibited from activities
while a claim is under consideration.
