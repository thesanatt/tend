Crime Victim Compensation Application

Content_Area1

If you were the victim of a crime in Wisconsin, you may be eligible for compensation for certain expenses related to the crime.

​Apply as soon as possible. Do not wait until a court case is complete or treatment has ended.

Choose How You Want to Apply

Option 1: Apply Online (Recommended)

  • To apply, you must create an account with a personal email to log in and apply. You will receive an email confirmation to confirm your account and then you can log in to apply.​
  • Once you have applied, you will be able log into your account to check your status, view or download a copy of your submitted application, and submit documentation such as bills and receipts.​
 Apply Online Now

Option 2: Apply Using a Paper Application 

Choose a language below to download a printable application.

Select Your Language

  • ​​English Paper Application (PDF)​​​
  • Español​ Paper Application (PDF)
  • ​Hmong Paper application (PDF)​​

Submit Your Completed Paper Application

You may mail or fax your completed paper application.

​​Need Help?

If you need help completing​ an application or require an alternative format due to a disability, contact the Crime Victim Compensation Program.​

Once an application is received, a specialist will be assigned to your claim. Request for additional information such as employment or medical records may be made.
