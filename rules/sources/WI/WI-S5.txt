            Crime Victim Compensation Application Skip to main content Crime Victim Compensation Application Content_Area1 I f you were the victim of a crime in Wisconsin, you may be eligible for compensation for certain expenses related to the crime. ​Apply as soon as possible. Do not wait until a court case is complete or treatment has ended. Choose How You Want to Apply Option 1: Apply Online (Recommended) To apply, you must create an account with a personal email to log in and apply. You will receive an email confirmation to confirm your account and then you can log in to apply.​ Once you have applied, you will be able log into your account to check your status, view or download a copy of your submitted application, and submit documentation such as bills and receipts.​ Apply Online Now Option 2: Apply Using a Paper Application Choose a language below to download a printable application. Select Your Language ​​English Paper Application (PDF)​​​ Español​ Paper Application (PDF) ​ Hmong Paper application (PDF)​ ​ Submit Your Completed Paper Application You may mail or fax your completed paper application. Mail to: Wisconsin Crime Victim Compensation Program PO Box 7951 Madison, WI 53707-7951 OR Fax to: (608) 264-6368 Content_Area2 ​ ​Wisconsin Crime Victim Compensation Program PO Box 7951 Madison, WI 53707-7951 Toll Free (800) 446-6564 Fax (608) 264-6368 Email ocvs@wisdoj.gov ​ ​​Need Help? If you need help completing​ an application or require an alternative format due to a disability, contact the Crime Victim Compensation Program.​ Once an application is received, a specialist will be assigned to your claim.
