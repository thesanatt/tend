Application Form - Alabama Crime Victims Compensation Commissions Safety Exit Skip to content Eligibility To access the application, please read over the eligibility requirements  and click the check box below. Eligible Victims A person who suffers physical or mental harm or death as a result of criminally injurious conduct committed within the State of Alabama. An Alabama resident that suffers personal injury or death as a result of criminally injurious conduct that occurs in a state that does not have a crime victims compensation program. Eligible Claimaints A victim of criminally injurious conduct. A surviving spouse or the next of kin of a victim who has died because of criminally injurious conduct. A person authorized to act on behalf of a victim. Additional Requirements The claim must be filed within one (1) year of the incident, or good cause must be submitted in writing. The crime must be reported to the proper law enforcement agency within 72 hours of the incident, or good cause must be submitted in writing. The victim or claimant must cooperate fully with law enforcement. The victim or claimant must not be the offender or an accomplice of the offender. No portion of the compensation shall unjustly benefit the offender. The victim must not have contributed to the offense. The victim will not be compensated if he/she has committed a criminal injury to another or he/she shall be convicted of a felony after applying for compensation. Must be a U.S. citizen or alien eligible for public benefits. Click here to view a list of documents accepted as proof of legal presence .
