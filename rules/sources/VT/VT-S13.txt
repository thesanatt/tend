Policy for Housing and Relocation
The intention of the Victims Compensation Program is to assist
victims with relocation costs in cases where a victim’s safety or
security are at risk or when a victim becomes displaced as a
direct result of a crime. If a victim is already working with the
Department of Children and Families Economic Services Division
we would ask that they contact their caseworker to find out if
they qualify for any rental assistance before the Victims
Compensation Program will consider payment. The Victims
Compensation Program will generally pay relocation costs no
more than once in a 12-month period.
Rent
Two months rent provided the victim is able to sustain payment
of the rent after the two months. The Compensation staff will
have discretion to determine what is considered as “reasonable
rent” based on the area in which the victim is relocating to.
Security Deposits
Security deposits not to exceed the cost of one month’s rent. A
security deposit cannot be used as last month’s rent. Last
month’s rent will not be considered. Landlord must sign an
agreement to reimburse the security deposit to the Victims
Compensation Program at the time that the premises are
vacated. If a victim has prepaid the security deposit prior to
application with the Victims Compensation Program, we will
reimburse the victim for the security deposit upon the victim’s
submission of a landlord’s receipt and a signed copy of the lease.
The victim must also sign an agreement to reimburse the security
deposit to the Victims Compensation Program at the time that the
premises are vacated.

Start-up Costs
Start-up costs could include the following: deposits for gas and
electric utility connection; basic land line phone connection; initial
post office box fee. Start-up costs not to exceed $500.00.
Relocation Costs
Relocation costs could include the following: rental trucks,
moving companies, transportation costs, storage fees not to
exceed three months. Total relocation costs not to exceed
$1,500.
Emergency Housing
Emergency housing will be paid at a maximum of $100.00 per
night, for not more than 7 nights.
Payment of Law Enforcement Fees
The Victims Compensation Program will consider payment for a
sheriff, deputy sheriff, or municipal law enforcement official to be
present at a suspect's/victim's residence to ensure a victim's
safety while removing their belongings. The Program will not pay
for fees when a suspect is removing their belongings from a
victim's residence. This fee will be paid in addition to the $1,500
relocation cap.
