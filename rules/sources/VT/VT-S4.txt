Types of crimes that may qualify for Victim Compensation: Arson Assault Burglary Child Physical Abuse/Neglect Child Sexual Abuse Material Child Sexual Abuse Domestic Violence DUI (Driving under influence of intoxicating liquor or other substance) Fraud/Financial crimes Homicide Human Trafficking Kidnapping Other Vehicular Crimes Robbery Sexual Assault Stalking Terrorism Other Expenses the Victims Compensation Program may pay for: Where appropriate, money is available to pay for the following expenses, that have not already been paid by other sources. The Victims Compensation Program determines what crime related losses can and cannot be paid by the Program on your claim and there are caps or limits on some types of losses. Medical/dental Mental health counseling for victim and supportive counseling for family members that live in the household with the victim Funeral costs Lost wages due to time missed from work Mileage/gas reimbursement Crime scene clean-up Limited rent/relocation Safety/security Child care Boarding of pets Payment for pets that are injured/killed during the crime If death occurs as a result of the crime, legal dependents may receive temporary living expenses Loss of support Eyeglasses, hearing aids, dentures, or any prosthetic device taken, lost or destroyed during the commission of a crime Expenses that cannot be paid by the Compensation Program: Any expense not related to the crime Any expenses paid by insurance or another source of reimbursement or coverage Expenses for lost, stolen or damaged property (except medically necessary items due to a qualifying crime) Damages for pain and suffering Attorney fees Click this button to leave the website immediately.
