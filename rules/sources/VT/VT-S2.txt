Financial assistance may be available to pay for your crime related losses. The Victims Compensation Program provides limited financial assistance to victims of crime. The financial loss has to be the direct result of the crime, and is not reimbursable through other sources, such as insurance. The application is available online as well as an in a fillable PDF in other languages. Click on the Compensation Applications & Forms tab to locate them and to learn more.
