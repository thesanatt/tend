Application & Forms

 Here you will find the application and all necessary documents to apply for victims compensation.

If you have questions, please call 800-750-1213 (Vermont only) or 802-241-1250. Our Compensation Claims Specialists are available Monday through Friday from 8:30am to 4:30pm.

If you have difficulties downloading or printing any of the documents, please call us.

When applying for Victims Compensation you do not have to provide a police report. The claims specialist assigned to your application will gather the information needed for eligibility.

Watch a short video on how to fill out an application for the Victims Compensation Program.  This video provides ASL interpretation.

The application is also available and can be downloaded in other languages, as well as in a fillable PDF version if you prefer to print and mail your application to us.
