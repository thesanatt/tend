BILLING INSTRUCTIONS:
Kansas Crime Victims Compensation (CVCB) is the first payer for the sexual assault forensic examination charge (K.S.A. 65-448(h)). Medical providers shall not bill
victims or their insurance for the sexual assault forensic examination. The CVCB will only pay for the exam, not the medical treatment, of sexual assault victims.
All other medical charges should be billed to the victim or their insurance carrier. Please provide the victim with a Crime Victims Compensation Board application.
They may apply whether or not they report the incident to law enforcement. All claims must be submitted for payment within 90 days of the date of the exam. For
payments, please submit an itemized invoice (including CPT codes if available), and this form to:
